How TopAI calculates these metrics

A conversation is a lead with an inbound text or a completed voice call. Appointments exclude cancelled appointments. Clients include Active Client and Closed Won statuses. ROI is shown as the gross revenue multiple: recorded GCI divided by recorded lead cost. Figures are all-time and only include leads tied to an external source.
