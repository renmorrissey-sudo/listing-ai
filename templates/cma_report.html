How this CMA was calculated

{% if report.analysis.valuation_method == "provider_avm" %}The indicated value and range come directly from the RentCast automated valuation model, which evaluates the exact location, subject attributes, nearby listing-market evidence, distance, and comparable correlation. TopAI retained the {{ report.analysis.selected_count }} highest-correlated eligible comparables from {{ report.analysis.avm_comparable_count }} AVM candidates. It does not extrapolate distant luxury-home price per square foot to the subject.{% else %}TopAI evaluated {{ report.analysis.candidate_count }} candidate sales and retained the {{ report.analysis.selected_count }} closest matches. The size-adjusted indication applies each selected sale's price per square foot to the target property's {{ number(report.criteria.sqft) }} square feet, then weights those indications by similarity.{% endif %}

Important CMA disclosure

This is a comparative market analysis, not an appraisal or guarantee of market value. RentCast AVM comparable prices and reference dates may come from active or inactive sale listings and are not automatically verified closed-sale amounts. Public records can lag county recordings. Verify status, final sale price, concessions, condition, geographic boundaries, and all material facts in the local MLS and public records before presenting or relying on this report.
