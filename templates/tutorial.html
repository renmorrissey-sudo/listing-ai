Tool 1

Listing Generator

Use this when you need listing copy, social posts, and a prospect email from one property.

Step-by-step

  1. Open the app and choose Listing Generator.
  2. Enter the property address, price, beds, baths, and square footage.
  3. Add optional details: year built, garage, pool, key features, and neighborhood highlights.
  4. Click Generate Content.
  5. Review the three tabs: Listing, Social Posts, and Email.
  6. Click Copy on any draft, then paste it into your MLS, CRM, email tool, or social scheduler.

What each output is for

  • Listing: MLS-style property description draft.
  • Social Posts: Platform-ready promotional copy for Instagram, Facebook, and X.
  • Email: Short prospect announcement with a subject line and call to action.
Tip: The more specific your features and neighborhood notes are, the stronger the drafts will sound. Always review AI copy before publishing.

Tool 2

Cold Call Scripts

Use this to prepare prospecting conversations before you call or leave a voicemail.

Step-by-step

  1. Choose Cold Call Scripts in the tool navigation.
  2. Enter the target area or neighborhood.
  3. Select the property type and seller situation (for example Expired Listing or FSBO).
  4. Add your name and a key benefit to mention.
  5. Click Generate Scripts.
  6. Review the tabs: Opening, Objections, and Voicemail.
  7. Copy the sections you want and keep them handy while prospecting.

How to use the scripts

  • Opening: Start the conversation and establish why you are calling.
  • Objections: Respond to common pushback without sounding pushy.
  • Voicemail: Leave a short, callback-friendly message when no one answers.
Tip: Update the key benefit for each farm area so the script feels local and personal.

Tool 3

AI Calling Assistant

Use this for consent-based, one-to-one follow-up calls. The AI identifies itself as an AI assistant and returns a summary when the call ends.

Before you start

  • Save your business profile (agent name, plus brokerage and/or company). These values are sent to the AI on every call.
  • Confirm the lead gave permission to be contacted.
  • Check Do Not Call, brokerage policy, and recording consent rules for your area.
  • Use this for one-to-one follow-up, not bulk robocalling.

Step-by-step

  1. Choose AI Calling Assistant.
  2. Save your business profile if you have not already.
  3. Select a persona, such as ISA / New Lead Follow-up or Open House Follow-up.
  4. Enter the lead name and phone number.
  5. Add call purpose, property interest, desired outcome, and lead context.
  6. Check the compliance confirmation box.
  7. Click Start Call.
  8. Watch Recent Calls for status updates, summaries, appointment indicators, and recording access.

How to read Recent Calls

  • Status: Shows whether the call is started, completed, or failed.
  • Summary: Appears after the provider webhook finishes processing the call.
  • Appointment requested: Highlights when the conversation points to a next step.
  • Recording: Opens a secure playback link when a recording is available.
Tip: Put the desired outcome in plain language, such as “qualify and book a buyer consultation.” That helps the AI stay focused.

Tool 4

AI SMS Assistant

Use this for consent-based one-to-one text outreach. Generate a short SMS, edit it, then send through Twilio when configured. When a lead replies, Claude coaches you — you always approve before anything is sent or applied.

Before you start

  • Confirm the lead consented to SMS contact.
  • Follow TCPA, DNC, carrier, and brokerage rules.
  • Include opt-out language when required.

Send an outbound SMS

  1. Choose AI SMS Assistant.
  2. Select a persona and enter the lead phone number.
  3. Add lead type, property interest, desired outcome, and notes.
  4. Click Generate SMS, then edit the draft if needed.
  5. Confirm compliance and click Send SMS.
  6. Sending creates or updates a CRM lead for that phone number.

When a lead replies

  1. Open Needs your review in the AI SMS panel (also surfaced on Dashboard and Needs Attention).
  2. Read Claude’s summary, intent, recommended next action, and draft reply.
  3. Edit the draft if needed, confirm consent, then click Approve & Send.
  4. If Claude suggests a status, follow-up, tasks, or appointment, use those Approve buttons separately — they do not apply until you click them.
  5. Use View conversation or Open CRM lead to see the full thread and lead record.

Opt-out and escalations

  • If the lead texts STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT, TopAI marks them Do Not Contact, cancels pending draft replies, and blocks further SMS.
  • Legal, financing, negotiation, fair housing, complaint, or uncertain property-fact topics require manual review before you can send.
Tip: TopAI never auto-sends AI replies and never auto-applies Claude’s CRM suggestions. You stay in control of every message and every pipeline change.

CRM

CRM & Lead Workflow

Manage every SMS lead in one place: statuses, follow-ups, tasks, appointments, a Needs Attention queue, and a pipeline view on the Dashboard. Times are stored in UTC and shown in your browser’s local time where formatted.

Where to find CRM

  • Dashboard — pipeline stage counts, overdue follow-ups, drafts awaiting approval, and Needs Attention highlights.
  • Leads — filterable lead list; open any lead for the full record.
  • Tasks — overdue, due today, and upcoming work.
  • Needs Attention — prioritized queue of items that need you now.
  • AI SMS — conversation history plus Claude approve cards.

Lead statuses

Each lead has a status you control. Change it from the lead detail page.

  • New → Attempting Contact → Contacted → Qualified
  • Appointment Scheduled → Appointment Completed
  • Nurture → Under Contract → Closed Won / Closed Lost
  • Do Not Contact — protected; automation cannot remove it after an opt-out

Every status change is written to the lead’s activity timeline.

Lead detail page

  1. Open Leads, then click Open on a lead.
  2. Use quick actions: Draft SMS, Call, create a task, schedule a follow-up, or schedule an appointment.
  3. Change status with the status dropdown and Save status.
  4. Scroll the activity timeline for SMS events, Claude insights, status changes, and completed work.

Follow-ups

  1. On the lead page, use quick picks: Tomorrow, +3 days, +1 week, +2 weeks, or +30 days.
  2. Add a short reason so you know why you’re circling back.
  3. Mark complete or dismiss when done. Overdue follow-ups appear in Needs Attention.

Tasks

  1. Create tasks from the lead page or from Tasks (call, send SMS, CMA, consultation, and more).
  2. Set due date and priority when needed.
  3. Complete or cancel from the Tasks page or the lead record. Completing a task logs activity on the lead.

Appointments

  1. Schedule an appointment on the lead page (type + start time).
  2. After the meeting, record an outcome (for example follow-up required, showing scheduled, not ready).
  3. Past appointments without an outcome show up in Needs Attention until you record one.

Needs Attention queue

Open this page daily. Items are prioritized (urgent / high first) and can include:

  • Unreviewed inbound SMS or draft replies awaiting approval
  • High intent, appointment requested, sensitive topic, or low AI confidence
  • Overdue follow-ups or tasks
  • Failed / undelivered SMS
  • Missing appointment outcomes
  • Opt-out requests

Click Resolve when handled. Opt-out, delivery failure, and sensitive-topic items require a short resolution reason.

Approving Claude CRM suggestions

  1. In the AI SMS inbox, review each suggestion card (status, follow-up, tasks, appointment).
  2. Click Approve only for the items you want applied.
  3. Leave the rest dismissed or handle them manually on the lead page.
Tip: Start each day on Dashboard → Needs Attention, clear the queue, then work your Tasks and follow-ups. Keep Do Not Contact leads untouched for SMS.

Recommended workflow

  1. Generate listing, social, and email drafts for a property.
  2. Publish or send the reviewed marketing copy.
  3. Generate call scripts for your target farm or lead type.
  4. Use AI Calling Assistant for consented follow-up calls.
  5. Use AI SMS Assistant for short consented text follow-ups (creates CRM leads).
  6. When leads reply, approve Claude’s draft SMS and any status / follow-up / task / appointment suggestions you want.
  7. Work the Needs Attention queue, complete tasks, and record appointment outcomes.
  8. Watch pipeline stages on the Dashboard as leads move toward Closed Won.
