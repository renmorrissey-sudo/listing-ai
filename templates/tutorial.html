How to use your TopAI tools

This tutorial is for paid subscribers. Follow each section to generate listing marketing content, prospecting scripts, and consent-based AI follow-up calls.

Tool 1

Listing Generator

Use this when you need listing copy, social posts, and a prospect email from one property.

Step-by-step

  1. Open the app and choose Listing Generator.
  2. Enter the property address, price, beds, baths, and square footage.
  3. Add optional details: year built, garage, pool, key features, and neighborhood highlights.
  4. Click Generate Content.
  5. Review the three tabs: Listing, Social Posts, and Email.
  6. Click Copy on any draft, then paste it into your MLS, CRM, email tool, or social scheduler.

What each output is for

  • Listing: MLS-style property description draft.
  • Social Posts: Platform-ready promotional copy for Instagram, Facebook, and X.
  • Email: Short prospect announcement with a subject line and call to action.
Tip: The more specific your features and neighborhood notes are, the stronger the drafts will sound. Always review AI copy before publishing.

Tool 2

Cold Call Scripts

Use this to prepare prospecting conversations before you call or leave a voicemail.

Step-by-step

  1. Choose Cold Call Scripts in the tool navigation.
  2. Enter the target area or neighborhood.
  3. Select the property type and seller situation (for example Expired Listing or FSBO).
  4. Add your name and a key benefit to mention.
  5. Click Generate Scripts.
  6. Review the tabs: Opening, Objections, and Voicemail.
  7. Copy the sections you want and keep them handy while prospecting.

How to use the scripts

  • Opening: Start the conversation and establish why you are calling.
  • Objections: Respond to common pushback without sounding pushy.
  • Voicemail: Leave a short, callback-friendly message when no one answers.
Tip: Update the key benefit for each farm area so the script feels local and personal.

Tool 3

AI Calling Assistant

Use this for consent-based, one-to-one follow-up calls. The AI identifies itself as an AI assistant and returns a summary when the call ends.

Before you start

  • Confirm the lead gave permission to be contacted.
  • Check Do Not Call, brokerage policy, and recording consent rules for your area.
  • Use this for one-to-one follow-up, not bulk robocalling.

Step-by-step

  1. Choose AI Calling Assistant.
  2. Select a persona, such as ISA / New Lead Follow-up or Open House Follow-up.
  3. Enter the lead name and phone number.
  4. Add lead type, property interest, desired outcome, and any agent notes.
  5. Check the compliance confirmation box.
  6. Click Start Call.
  7. Watch Recent Calls for status updates, summaries, appointment indicators, and recording access.

How to read Recent Calls

  • Status: Shows whether the call is started, completed, or failed.
  • Summary: Appears after the provider webhook finishes processing the call.
  • Appointment requested: Highlights when the conversation points to a next step.
  • Recording: Opens a secure playback link when a recording is available.
Tip: Put the desired outcome in plain language, such as “qualify and book a buyer consultation.” That helps the AI stay focused.

Recommended workflow

  1. Generate listing, social, and email drafts for a property.
  2. Publish or send the reviewed marketing copy.
  3. Generate call scripts for your target farm or lead type.
  4. Use AI Calling Assistant for consented follow-up calls.
  5. Review summaries and book the appointments that come back.
