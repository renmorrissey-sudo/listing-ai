How {{ product_name }} works

{{ product_name }} is operated by {{ business_name }}. It is a paid SaaS web app that helps real estate professionals create marketing and prospecting content with AI, and optionally place consent-based AI follow-up calls.

Customers subscribe through Stripe, open the web app, verify access with their subscriber email, enter property or lead details, and receive draft outputs they can copy, review, and use in their own real estate workflow.

Step 2

Access the web app

After payment, the customer visits the app homepage and enters the same email address used at Stripe checkout.

  1. Open topairealestatetools.com
  2. Enter the subscriber email on the access screen
  3. The app verifies that the email belongs to an active or eligible subscription
  4. Subscriber tools unlock for that session
Step 3

Generate listing and marketing drafts

In the Listing Generator, the agent fills in property details and clicks Generate Content.

  • AI creates a listing description draft
  • AI creates social posts for major platforms
  • AI creates a prospect email with a subject line and call to action
  • The agent reviews the drafts and copies the text they want to use
Step 4

Create prospecting scripts

In Cold Call Scripts, the agent enters area, property type, seller situation, and value proposition, then generates opening, objection, and voicemail scripts for review.

Step 5

Optional: run consent-based AI calls

In AI Calling Assistant, the agent confirms consent, enters lead details, and starts a one-to-one AI call. After the call ends, Recent Calls shows status, summary, outcome, and recording access when available.

  • Intended for consent-based, one-to-one real estate follow-up
  • The AI identifies itself as an AI assistant
  • Customers remain responsible for calling and recording compliance
