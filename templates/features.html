Listing Generator

Creates MLS-style property marketing drafts from the listing details the agent provides.

What you enter

  • Property address, price, beds, baths, and square footage
  • Garage, pool, and other property attributes
  • Feature highlights and neighborhood notes

What you get

  • A polished listing description draft
  • Platform-ready social posts for Instagram, Facebook, and X/Twitter
  • A prospect email with subject line and call to action
  • One-click copy buttons for each output

Cold Call Script Generator

Builds real estate prospecting scripts tailored to a target market and outreach goal.

What you enter

  • Target area and property type
  • Seller or prospect situation
  • Agent name and key value proposition

What you get

  • Opening call script
  • Objection handling language
  • Short voicemail script

AI SMS Assistant

Drafts consent-based one-to-one SMS follow-ups from lead details. Agents can edit the message before sending through the configured SMS provider when credentials are set.

What you enter

  • Persona, lead name, and phone number
  • Lead type, property interest, and desired outcome
  • Optional agent notes and compliance confirmation

What you get

  • AI-generated SMS draft ready to edit
  • Recent SMS history with sent/draft/failed status
  • Optional live sending through the configured SMS provider (Telnyx in production)

AI Calling Assistant

Starts consent-based, one-to-one AI follow-up calls for real estate lead outreach. The assistant identifies itself as AI and stores call outcomes for the subscriber to review.

What you enter

  • Call persona (for example ISA / new lead follow-up)
  • Lead name, phone number, lead type, and property interest
  • Desired outcome and agent notes
  • Explicit confirmation that the lead may be contacted

What you get

  • Outbound AI call started through the configured voice provider
  • Call status, summary, and outcome in Recent Calls
  • Transcript and recording access when available
  • Appointment-request indicators when the conversation points to a next step

Customers remain responsible for TCPA, Do Not Call, recording consent, brokerage policy, and local compliance before placing any call.
